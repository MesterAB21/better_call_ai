Better Call AI is an AI-powered legal research platform designed to make Algerian legislation more accessible, understandable, and actionable. It helps users explore legal texts, understand complex legal situations, and obtain contextualized consultations grounded in Algerian law.

Covering legislation from **1962 to the present**, Better Call AI combines intelligent legal search, a multi-agent AI system, and structured legal knowledge to help users navigate the complexity of the Algerian legal framework.

## ✨ Key Features

- 🔎 **Intelligent Legal Search** — Search legislation and legal provisions using natural-language questions.
- 🤖 **11 Parallel AI Sub-Agents** — Specialized agents collaborate in parallel to support complex legal research and information retrieval.
- ✅ **Multi-Layer Verification** — Multiple verification layers help assess the relevance, consistency, and legal grounding of information before it is presented to the user.
- 📝 **Interactive Legal Questionnaires** — Generate targeted questions to better understand the user's circumstances, identify missing information, and provide a more relevant, context-aware consultation.
- 🔗 **Legal Relationship Tracking** — Handle complex relationships between legal provisions, including amendments, replacements, repeals, and abrogations, helping consultations reflect the applicable and up-to-date legal framework.
- 🧠 **Context-Aware Consultations** — Ground responses in relevant legal sources and the specific details of the user's situation.
- 📚 **Extensive Algerian Legal Knowledge Base** — Explore a large collection of legal publications, legislative texts, and legal articles.
- 📅 **Historical and Current Legislation** — Support legal research across different periods of Algerian legislation, from 1962 to the present.

## 🧩 Architecture Highlights

- **Parallel multi-agent processing:** 11 specialized sub-agents support complex legal research.
- **Hybrid information retrieval:** Combines complementary search approaches to discover relevant legal material.
- **Relevance ranking:** Reranking techniques help prioritize the most pertinent legal passages.
- **Multi-layer verification:** Additional checks help improve the reliability and legal grounding of generated responses.
- **Legal dependency and evolution awareness:** Relationships between articles are considered to account for modifications, replacements, and repeals.
- **Situation-aware consultation:** Targeted questionnaires help clarify the facts before generating a more relevant response.
